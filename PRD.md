IMMERSIVE LOCATION ENGINE — MAURITANIA IRON ORE TRAIN


---

FIRST PRINCIPLES

Scale
Immense, horizontal, and unforgiving.
The train stretches over 2.5 kilometers—longer than most villages it passes.
The landscape is infinite desert—no edges, no shelter, no relief.

Tempo
Slow, relentless, mechanical.
It does not rush, but it never stops unless it must.
Time stretches—measured in wind, heat, and distance, not minutes.

Emotion
Exposure. Endurance. Humility.
A human feels small, temporary, and intensely alive against something vast and indifferent.

Silence
Not true silence—
Wind roars. Metal groans. Sand whispers.
What’s absent is human noise: no chatter, no city hum, no interruption.
Only elemental sound.


---

THE EXPERIENCE — “RIDING THE IRON LINE”


---

1. ARRIVAL — THE EDGE OF STILLNESS

The screen opens in near-black.

A faint, low-frequency rumble emerges—not heard, but felt.

A thin horizontal line appears.

Not drawn—revealed.

It slowly becomes the horizon.

No UI. No text.

Just:

A vast desert at dawn

Cold blue light

Wind barely audible


Then—far in the distance—
A moving line.

Too long to comprehend.

The train.


---

2. ORIENTATION — UNDERSTANDING SCALE

The user does not “control a camera.”

They stand.

A subtle shift (mouse / touch) mimics head movement.

The train approaches—but slowly enough that the user questions if it’s moving at all.

Key design:

No minimap

No indicators

Scale is understood through waiting


As the train nears:

Individual wagons become visible

Then their repetition becomes overwhelming

Then counting becomes impossible


A quiet realization emerges:

> This is not an object. It is a system.




---

3. BOARDING — TRANSITION WITHOUT CUT

No button says “board.”

Instead:

As the train passes, a single open ore wagon aligns with the user.

The wind intensifies.

Sound deepens.

If the user leans forward (scroll / gesture),
they are absorbed into motion.

No cut.

No fade.

Just continuity.

Now:

You are inside the train.


---

4. EXPLORATION — MOVEMENT AS ENDURANCE

Movement is not free roaming.

It is constrained like reality:

You can shift within a wagon

You can climb slightly

You can look across wagons

You cannot “jump” freely


The desert moves endlessly.

The horizon barely changes.

Environmental dynamics:

Wind increases with speed

Sand occasionally sweeps across the screen

Light transitions slowly (day → dusk → night)


Micro-interactions:

Looking down reveals iron ore texture—rough, granular

Looking back shows infinite wagons fading into dust

Looking forward shows the locomotive barely visible


No UI labels.

But occasionally, whisper text fades in and out:

> “700 kilometers”
“Zouérat to Nouadhibou”
“One of the longest trains on Earth”



Then it disappears.

Like a thought, not a fact.


---

5. DISCOVERY — HUMAN TRACES

After time passes:

You notice something unexpected.

A piece of cloth tied to metal.
A footprint in ore dust.
A distant silhouette of another rider.

These are not highlighted.

They are found.

If the user focuses:

Soft text appears:

> “People ride this for survival.”
“12+ hours exposed to the desert.”



No narration. No explanation.

Just fragments.


---

6. TIME DISTORTION MECHANIC

The longer the user stays:

Motion feels slower

Sound deepens

Visual contrast softens


This is intentional.

The experience subtly simulates fatigue and adaptation.

Not through UI—through perception.


---

7. NIGHT — THE VOID

Eventually:

Darkness.

No stars at first.

Just black.

Then gradually:

A sky explodes into visibility.

The train continues.

Now the dominant sound is metal rhythm.

The user feels suspended in nothing.


---

8. REFLECTION — STILLNESS INSIDE MOTION

At any moment, if the user becomes completely still:

Everything responds:

Wind softens

Sound narrows

The horizon stabilizes


The world does not stop.

But it acknowledges presence.

This is the only “control” the user truly has.


---

VISUAL LANGUAGE

Color Palette

Iron red (ore)

Sand gold

Washed sky blue

Night black


Muted. Dust-filtered. Never saturated.

Typography

Thin, humanist sans-serif

Appears briefly, fades gently

Never anchored to UI elements


Geometry

No sharp UI boxes

Soft overlays that feel like الهواء (air), not interface



---

MOTION PRINCIPLES

No loops

Everything progresses forward

Movement always tied to train velocity


Transitions are never cuts—only continuations.


---

AUDIO DESIGN

Wind (primary layer)

Metal friction + rhythm

Occasional deep engine resonance


No music.

Ever.

Silence emerges only when the user becomes still.


---

WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED (INTENTIONALLY)

No maps

No progress bars

No achievements

No “learn more” buttons

No voiceover


Nothing that breaks presence.


---

FINAL EXPERIENCE TRUTH

This is not about a train.

It is about:

Distance without interruption

Movement without control

Survival without comfort



---

VALIDATION

If a user finishes this experience and later sees a photo of the Mauritania train…

They should feel it in their body:

The wind.
The scale.
The waiting.

And think:

> “I’ve been there.”
